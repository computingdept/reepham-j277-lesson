# Reepham College OCR J277 lesson hub

A classroom lesson hub for OCR GCSE Computer Science J277.

- **Year 10:** J277Lesson001–J277Lesson072
- **Year 11:** J277Lesson073–J277Lesson142

The lesson numbers follow the current Year 10 and Year 11 schemes of learning. Half-term groupings are adjustable for future school calendars, assessment time, reteaching and examination arrangements.
